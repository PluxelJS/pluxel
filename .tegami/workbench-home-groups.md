## Grouped plugins and apps on the Workbench home

The home page lists plugins by the same catalog groups as the plugins page:
ungrouped plugins first, then each group under a heading that folds it away,
with plugins needing attention first inside each list. App shortcuts are
grouped by the Workbench `group` their tab or route navigation declares, so the
pages of related plugins sit together.
